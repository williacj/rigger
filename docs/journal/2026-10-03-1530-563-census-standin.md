ABOUTME: Journal for #563 (M4-F17): why the census stand-in in `test/exit-cleanup.test.mjs`'s loop
at `:920` sometimes never marked a read, and why warming it is the fix.

# #563 — the census stand-in's first exec was the cleanup's first read

**The cause.** In the loop's two tests, nothing runs the stand-in for `ps` before SIGTERM. A call
with no `onGroup` reads no start time, so the exit cleanup's first census read of group 1 is the
stand-in's first exec. The system holds the first exec of a file just written until it has
assessed it, as `warmed` in `test/process-fixtures.mjs` already records. Where that hold outlasts
the read timeout, `spawnSync` kills the stand-in before its first line. The cleanup then refuses
every later read of that `ps` (`unanswered`, read in `runNow`), so the census and the reads after
the kill all fail without running it. Nothing marks, the group is still killed unnamed, and only
the guard fails.

**The evidence.** It comes from a probe in a scratch clone at `27be550`, never committed. The probe
logged every synchronous read the cleanup made, and every stand-in exec that reached its first
line. The figures below are measurements, taken with Node 26.5.0 on macOS 27.0 on 2026-10-03.

- Over 10 runs at a one-minute load of 23.7 to 27.2, the first read of `ps-partial` took 50 to
  65 ms, and its second took 12 to 28 ms. For `ps-silent` the first took 46 to 60 ms and the second
  7 to 12 ms. In every run the first read was the cleanup's census read of group 1, its first
  `-ww -g`.
- With the read timeout lowered to 40 ms, 3 of 3 runs failed both tests at the guard. Each run's
  first read ended `ETIMEDOUT` after 41 to 43 ms. No stand-in exec reached its first line, and
  every later read failed with `LATE`, or with "earlier in the exit cleanup, which does not wait on
  it again".
- With the stand-in warmed first, at the same 40 ms timeout, both tests passed 3 of 3 times.

The failed run on #561 logged no reads, so the cause is inferred there, not measured. Its
`ps-partial` test took 3,954 ms against `ps-silent`'s 2,130 ms, at a load of 27 to 31. A census of
`ps-partial` with no hold takes well under a second. The 2,000 ms gap is consistent with a hold
that ran the 2,000 ms read out. `warmed`'s own note measured one first exec in 150 at 1,058 ms at a
load of about 33, so a hold past 2,000 ms at load 30 is the tail of the same distribution. That
last step is a judgment.

**The `:921`–`:924` suspicion was the cause.** It suspected that the first read timed out before
the stand-in marked it, and it did. Raising the timeout from 300 ms to 2,000 ms only made the hold
rarer to outlast. The comment now says what was measured.

**The fix.** `endCaller` takes an optional `warm`, which runs the stand-in that `options.ps`
names once before the caller starts. The two tests pass it, so the hold is paid with no bound on
it. No bound changed, and the guard stays.

**The forcing.** `c563-force.mjs` in the coordinator's scratchpad holds the first exec of
`ps-partial` and `ps-silent`, whichever process makes it, until the caller has ended. It sits above
the warming line, because the system's hold comes before a script's first line. Where the caller
made that exec, the stand-in blocks on `ps-hold` until the cleanup's own 2,000 ms bound kills it.
Where the test made it, as `warm` does before any caller exists, it goes on at once. The forcing
uses no sleep and no timer of its own.

**`ps-quit` shares the cause.** The survivor test's stand-in, `ps-quit`, is unwarmed too, so its
first exec is also the cleanup's first census read. Under a coordinator grant for `:891`, that test
passes `warm: true` as well. With the forcing widened to `ps-quit`, the test failed at its guard,
"the child was never told to go", in 3 of 3 runs at the base, at loads 13.47, 13.43 and 12.75. It
passed in 3 of 3 at the head, each at a load of 11.93.

**Five more tests share it.** The engineer judge found five more unwarmed census stand-ins whose
first exec is the cleanup's first read: `ps-join`, `ps-late`, `ps-join-fails`, `ps-hides` and
`ps-gone`. Each test's `endCaller` call passes `warm: true` under a coordinator grant. With the
forcing widened to them, all five failed in each of 3 runs at the base, at loads 23.17, 16.24 and
12.08. Four failed at their guards. `ps-hides` has no guard, and it failed because the child its
census hides went unnamed. All five passed in each of 3 runs at the head.
