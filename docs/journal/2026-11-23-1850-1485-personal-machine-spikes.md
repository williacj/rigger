# Personal machine spikes

`docs/host-bootstrap.md` owns what a spike on a personal machine may collect and publish. The guide
binds nobody by itself, so the spike engineer's prompt and the acceptance and code-review skills
carry the pointers that give it force.

The live word pool is nearly full, so the pointers were paid for with sentences that explained a
rule and held none of their own. `npm run budget:instructions` measured 15,284 live words at
`main`'s 6798fdf1, and 15,282 on this change, against a limit of 15,300.
