# #558 — refusing the three names that name no directory of their own

**The refusal sits beside the `/` refusal in `readRoles`, and needs nothing from the kinds.** A
kind that names one of the three as its maker or a judge names a role the config declares, so the
role refusal fires the same way whatever the kind says. That is why the card's kind item holds
without its own code.

**A `git checkout -- <file>` restore after a mutation also undoes uncommitted work.** The first
round of mutations restored the source that way before the fix was committed. The second and third
mutations then ran against the base code. The anchor count (0) showed it, and those runs were
discarded and redone once the fix was committed. Commit before mutating, or restore from a saved
copy.
