# Private content stays unpublished in every session

`docs/host-bootstrap.md`, "Spikes on a personal machine", binds the cards that adopt it. Other
work publishes evidence as well, and `AGENTS.md` is the one file every session loads, so the rule
is there. The terms stay where they are defined, and the bullet refers to them.

A first wording said in its own words what may be published, and that contradicted the definition
beside it. A rule that borrows a term leaves the term's limits with its owner.

With the bullet, `AGENTS.md` counts 2,499 words of 2,500, from 2,469 at b23d656d
(`npm run budget:instructions` in this card's worktree on 2026-11-24, measured). One word is
spare, so a later change that adds more than one there must cut.
