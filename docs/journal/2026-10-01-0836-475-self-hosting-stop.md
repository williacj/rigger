# #475 — a stop that fits the pool by cutting what was already owned elsewhere

**The live instruction pool had 7 words of room.** `npm run budget:instructions` at `1983ba5`
counted 13993 live words against 14000. The Self-hosting stop takes 25 words by that script's
whitespace split, so it could not land without a cut.

**The cut was text another document already owns.** "Write the test first" and "Commit atomically
and often" retyped `AGENTS.md`'s "When you write code" and "Version control", which the engineer
prompt's opening keeps binding anyway. Removing them takes 21 words, and the head counts 13997.
Under O15 the card named those exact words in advance, so the maker chose no text to cut.

**The stop binds only a session that Rigger dispatched.** Its condition is in the prompt's own
words, "Dispatched by Rigger". A maker dispatched by hand keeps `D22`'s permission over a
Self-hosting path, and a judge reading the prompt finds no stop for that maker.
