Card #1155 asks for an author procedure using the existing checker. At source
`4f43b740fa3078a0913c9483d0dab39f6e145722`, `scripts/preflight.mjs` reads backticked
paths from a literal Lands in bullet and its indented continuation. `scripts/card-check.mjs`

The live skill and its distribution template move together. This instruction-only change
adds no parser, test, admission rule or test-health criterion from #1139.
