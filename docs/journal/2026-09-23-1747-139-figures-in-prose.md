## 2026-09-23 — Figures in prose carry their evidence

D16 governs code depending on facts owned by outside tools. It does not govern a number asserted
in a PR body, card, verdict, or journal entry. The owner ratified the #115 proposal to put that
writing rule in `AGENTS.md`, where every session reads it. A measured figure now identifies its
source, state, scope, and result; a judgment identifies its premise. Authors and reviewers apply
the rule without a new machine checker or a change to D16.
