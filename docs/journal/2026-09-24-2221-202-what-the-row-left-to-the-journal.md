# 2026-09-24 — What the row left to the journal

Card #195 put the priority field and its ranked options in the Engine settings row, and left two
gaps. It named no form for the declaration, so M1's config card would have had to invent the key.
It also left open whether a card holding an undeclared value ranks with a card holding none. Card
#195's journal entry said it did, and `ARCHITECTURE.md` did not.

A sentence that settles a question in the journal settles nothing, because the journal binds
nothing. The row now says both cases share one rank below every declared option, and that cards
sharing a rank are ordered by issue number, oldest first.

The form sits in a paragraph after the config shape block rather than inside it, because
`test/config.test.mjs` holds the validator to that block in both directions. It is worded to stay
true once M1's config card lands the key in the block. A line reading "the shape above does not
carry it" would have gone stale on that card, and been a line nobody owned deleting.

The stated line was checked by substituting it for the block's `board` line and importing the
result, the way the config test runs the block. It imports, and apart from `priority` its `board`
equals the published one.
