# D29 bounds a deterministic test's timers and asks a new host test why it needs the host

The owner directed on 2026-10-29, at
[740#6497180994](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/740#issuecomment-6497180994), that bad
tests be stopped before they become debt. The architect placed the two rules that direction needs
in `D29`, in [the ruling at 740#6497153704](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/740#issuecomment-6497153704),
§2, and corrected four points of it in
[the amendment at 740#6499745772](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/740#issuecomment-6499745772).

This card writes only the decision text, verbatim from the card at acceptance revision
`36fe22b9b890`: rule 3 gains a fifth bound, that a deterministic test arms no timer with a delay
above zero, and rule 11 asks a host test a change adds to declare its reason and the
deterministic test that proves its decision. Both rules land on one card because two cards
amending `D29` at once would conflict on its status line and on rule 3.

The runner for the timer bound is M6-32 (#1137), and rule 11's check is M6-33 (#1138). Neither
binds until the owner ratifies this delta by marker.
