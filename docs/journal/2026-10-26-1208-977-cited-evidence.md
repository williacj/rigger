# Card 977: an acceptance item may let the maker cite persistent evidence

[U77](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/740#issuecomment-6421782654), ruling 3, allowed
evidence to be cited rather than hand-copied where the cited record persists: a CI run, a posted
comment or a committed file, never a local log. This card writes that into the acceptance
skill's "Choose a format", after its default bullet shapes, where
[the card's attack](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/977#issuecomment-6422174200) placed
it. The skill states the rule on its own authority and cites no ruling, because `rigger init`
forks it into repositories where U77 refers to nothing.

The paragraph adds 59 words to the live instruction pool: `npm run budget:instructions` reports
14,778 at the merge base and 14,837 at the head, against the card's cap of 60.

What taught us: nine rulings in under sixty words leaves no room for a list, whose markers count
as words; one dense paragraph that a judge can quote clause by clause fits where a table cannot.
