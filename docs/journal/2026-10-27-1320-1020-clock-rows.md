# The clock rows

At `6b31ca74`, `R-SCHED-5`, `-6` and `-8` say that a schedule starts work and that a missed
trigger fires once or not at all. They leave open whether `rigger run` ends at the drain while a
clock is declared, what a trigger does while its earlier card is open or admission is closed,
whether `rigger once` fires one, when a new trigger first fires, and what an unreadable record
does. The owner settled those in [M7-U2](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1009#issuecomment-6434513387)
items 1, 2, 3, 5, 11, 12 and 13. This card writes them as rows, so the change cards build against
the register rather than against comments.

The structure under the rows is the architect's [r2 review](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1009#issuecomment-6434658018):
F1 (the board poll and its required interval), F3 (one card per occurrence across a restart) and
F4 (arming keyed on name and schedule), which [M7-K5](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1009#issuecomment-6434666807)
accepts. M7-K5 leaves two rows to the owner, `R-SCHED-30` and `R-SCHED-37`.
[M7-U4](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1009#issuecomment-6449422492) Q20 (c) adds a
third, `R-SCHED-43`, the skip of a card a run set aside. All three are copied from the card's
pinned fence word for word.

The ids come from [M7-K6](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1009#issuecomment-6440729098)
item 1, which gives this card `R-SCHED-28` to `R-SCHED-41` and #1021 `R-SCHED-42`. `R-SCHED-43`
was the next id free in both registers at `6b31ca74`.

No row claims a test. [M7-U3](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1009#issuecomment-6440291524)
item 2 (Q17 (a)) lets a spec row land before the change card whose test claims it, so the
regenerated matrix marks all fifteen a gap until M7-20 to M7-24 land.

One choice of form is the maker's and is flagged for the owner. `R-SCHED-32`'s "at most one card"
carries the limit the card's coverage map marks out of scope: a person's edit to the card before
the restart. The r2 review's §2 names that residual, and the row states it rather than promise
what the ratified structure does not deliver.

In round 1 the judges ruled that `R-SCHED-29`'s four applicability conditions and `R-SCHED-41`'s
three refusal cases sat in prose, against spec-style rule 4. Continuation rows would each take an
id the card never allocated, so each row now cites a dimensions table headed with its own id, as
the card's "Lands in" permits. The tables carry the same conditions and cases, so neither row's
meaning moves and both keep their ids.
