# The supervised re-run held its pause, then looped on a maker that opened no pull request

#1255 ran the candidate `76d33f169c180ee08abfde405ea05e16b59e57ae` under launchd on this laptop. It was installed from its own `npm pack` tarball at `/Users/cjwilliams/rigger-builds/m7-1255`, against a fresh clone at `/Users/cjwilliams/rigger-supervised/m7/target`, with #1256 as the one supervised card. The evidence is on #1255, and its private originals are under `/Users/cjwilliams/rigger-evidence/m7/76d33f169c180ee08abfde405ea05e16b59e57ae/1255`. This entry says what was learned. It quotes no evidence.

What held. A `rigger pause` taken inside #1256's first claim let the in-flight maker run on to its end. Sixteen later launchd invocations each observed the persisted owner closer and claimed nothing. `rigger resume` reopened admission, and the next invocation claimed #1256 again. The install root's SHA-256 listing was unchanged after the bootout. The protected checkout's content and index were unchanged too. Its ref and worktree differences all came from other named sessions. After the bootout, no invocation started.

What failed. Four makers ran on #1256. The three that ended exited 0 with a commit made, but none opened a pull request. Each time, L2 recorded `review.withheld` and the run exited 1. The next launchd run then re-claimed the card, removed its workspace, and dispatched a fresh maker. Every dispatch carried attempt 1, and nothing escalated, so the card could never reach Done or Owner. The owner booted the job out partway through the fourth claim (M7-U65). Two defects are named here, and their root-cause cards are filed separately: the unbounded re-claim loop after a maker exits 0 without a pull request, and the removal of the workspace that discards each maker's unpushed output.

What it cost the acceptance. Items 23, 25 and 26 are unmet, because no head was sound and #1256 ended in Coding. Item 16 is contested: the work ran to its end under the pause, but produced no pull request. Item 13 is contested: the `runs` read was taken before a fourth run, not immediately before the bootout. Item 2 is contested over the owner's install command form and the coordinator's read-only checks made outside the commands comment. The card escalates as `ambiguous`.

Lessons for the next window:

- A bootout meant to fall between claims needs an owner already waiting. The 60 s gap was missed, and a fresh claim was cut short.
- On this host, the Homebrew `claude` (2.1.309) did not answer `--version`, so the plist's `PATH` must put `~/.local/bin` first.
- A re-bootstrap resets launchd's `runs`. The stream's `run.start` count, not `runs`, is the window's tally.
