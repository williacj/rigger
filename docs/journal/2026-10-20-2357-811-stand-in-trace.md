# Card #811: a trace of three stand-in tests, and where it was forced to stop

Card #811 traces every run of three tests in `test/stand-in-agent.test.mjs`. They are "a run awaiting a file written after its first read found it missing, with no watch reporting the write, settles while the writer is still alive", "a run awaiting a file its writer fails to write does not settle, and ended by SIGTERM leaves no FIFO beside itself" and "a writer that fails to announce a file after the wait's first read found it missing fails saying why, and the wait does not settle". The card calls them `:177`, `:225` and `:389`. A run held at its 60 s bound now takes a snapshot at the `t.signal` abort and prints its whole trace. Every other run names the directory under `C615_EVIDENCE_ROOT` that holds its trace.

`lsof` given a FIFO's path lists no holder on macOS 27.0.1, though a listing by process names the FIFO by that path. Measured with `/usr/sbin/lsof` on 2026-10-20: a process holding both ends of a FIFO was listed by `lsof -p`, and `lsof -- <path>` exited 1 with no output. So the snapshot reads the holders of the gate and of the waiter's FIFO from one listing of every file the user's processes hold.

A reader blocked in `open()` of a FIFO counts as a reader on macOS. Measured on the same host: while a child was stopped inside a blocking read-open of a FIFO, a non-blocking write-open of it succeeded. So a waiter that has said `read false` lets the gate's write-open return before its own read-open does, and an `open()` held with the waiter holding no read end (S3a) means the write-open never reached a worker, or the waiter was not yet in its read-open.

Every stage the card names was forced, on throwaway trees, without a sleep. The trees and their runs are in the pull request:

| Test | Stage forced | How | Stage the trace classified |
|---|---|---|---|
| `:225` | S1 | the waiter stops before its first read, so it never says `read false` | S1 |
| `:225` | S2 | the writer blocks while it loads | S2 |
| `:225` | S3a | the threadpool is filled before the gate's open | S3a |
| `:225` | S3b | as S3a, with a helper holding the gate's write end | S3b |
| `:225` | S3c | the test opens the gate itself, stops the waiter, and writes 4 MiB | S3c |

| `:389` | W1, W2, W3a, W3c, W5 | as S1, S2, S3a, S3c and S5 | W1, W2, W3a, W3c, W5 |

Every counted attempt that was not forced passed, so each is classed passed and reached no stage. Those are 20 lone-file runs and one commit hook's full suite, all started at a one-minute load of 24 or less. Runs started above 24 are not counted, and they do not compare. At base, with no trace, six skip-lifted runs started at loads of 25.75 to 57.14, and three of them were cancelled at the bound: `:225` twice and `:389` once. They carried no trace. At head, seven skip-lifted runs started at loads of 25.86 to 34.24, and all of them passed. The pull request's ledger holds every run.

The first judging round found that the trace closed during teardown before Node reported the children the sweep had just killed. A run could end with a child's final reading null on both fields, and with no exit or close event. That held for every passing `:177` run, because its writer lives until teardown. `3b8913e` keeps the trace open until each child it started has closed. A throwaway checker that requires each spawned child's exit, close and non-null final reading failed on `b897bb2` and passed after the change.
