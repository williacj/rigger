## Carry items

- **Rigger**, mostly #1510:
  - a judge's scratch probes block L1;
  - a body-only answer is read as answering nothing;
  - the engine counts rounds per PR, so an owner's per-card grant isn't enforced;
  - a running run picks up mid-run Ready moves only sometimes;
  - launchd's StartInterval sometimes skips;
  - the loaded ExitTimeOut of 60 doesn't match the plist's 120;
  - a host build hangs on sccache unless `GGML_CCACHE=OFF` is set.
- **Host:** fseventsd growth (M8-K107). After a restart, notify latency had a median of 12 ms and a
  maximum of 20 ms over 12,562 writes.
- **Emend:** emend#225 to #229 (M8-U199), and emend#191, left open with K107's evidence.

## Lessons

- **Re-arm a waiter from the last event's line.** A waiter re-armed from a stale line missed two
  escalations. Stops 5 and 6 were reported late because of it.
- **Screen cards for `.claude/` paths before Ready.** A headless Claude maker can't write `.claude/`,
  and #98 escalated within a minute of its pull.
- **Check a merge path against the kind's panel before naming it.** M8-K109 first named an owner's
  marker for the re-pin, but Emend's `change` panel holds one reviewer, and a paused engine dispatches
  no judge. M8-U206 replaced it with a reviewer launched by hand.
