# Holding repeated host failures

Card #870 makes L2 compare the two failures of one card's attempt. When the same host kind fails
twice, L2 answers `hold` and records both failures with the decision. L3 hands back the first
failure as L2 answered it, including for a continuing attempt.

K52 on M6 record #740 is the interim L3 rule: a `hold` ends that card's attempts, names both
failures, and keeps the card out of later pulls in the same `run` invocation. The verb reports
that the card was held. L3 keeps the count only within an invocation.

This card claims neither closed admission, nor the card-local rows, nor the other causes that
M6-08e (#871) routes to `stop`. M6-08e separates those L1 reports; M6-08b then closes admission
on a hold.
