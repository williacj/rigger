ABOUTME: Records the root cause of R2's intermittent failure at #1467's round-1 head, and its premise fix.

# R2's premise accepted a read that showed M6 inside the group

## What failed

The architect judge's round-1 receipt for PR #1489 ran R2 with the source at the head, and R2
failed once: `first.alive` held M6. Further runs passed.

R2 was rerun at the same head with diagnostics added to its failure message: the record the dead
engine left, each logged whole-table read's row for M6, the restart's events, and M6's state
afterwards. Runs 1 to 25 used the original premise, and 3 of them failed (runs 14, 19 and 23, at
one-minute loads of 13.8 to 15.6). All three show the same thing:

- **Read 0 showed M6 inside the group.** It is the tracker's first read, which runs at once when
  the dispatch starts, while M1 is still forking. M6 had not yet called `setsid`, so its row read
  `[M6, M1, group]`.
- **Read 1 had not answered** when the engine was killed.
- **The record tracked only M3.**
- **The restart had no event naming M6**, and M6 was still alive afterwards, in a session of its own,
  with ppid 1.

## Why it failed

The premise asked only that a settled read showed M6 with M1 as its parent, and M1 in the group.
It did not ask that M6 be outside the group. The tracker adds only processes outside the group
(`ARCHITECTURE.md`, "Failure model"). So the premise held while the tracker had not added M6.

The test then wrote `parent-exit`. M1 exited before any later read showed M6 outside the group,
so no read ever attributed M6 to the dispatch.

Two other hypotheses were measured and set aside:

- **A zombie counted alive.** 200 orphans in sessions of their own were killed by SIGKILL at a
  one-minute load of 28.9. Each stopped answering signal 0 within 20.6 ms at most, and `ps` never
  showed one as a zombie.
- **A tracker read that fails to parse.** 300 whole-table reads, parsed as the tracker parses
  them, gave no failure.

## The fix

The premise now requires M6's row to have a process group other than the dispatch's. That is the
row the tracker adds. The failing runs' read 0 satisfied the old predicate and fails the new one.
