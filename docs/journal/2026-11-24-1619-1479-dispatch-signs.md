# Dispatch signs stop subsequent work

Card [#1479](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1479), acceptance revision
`9bf3ef0a668f`, makes `R-STATE-30` through `R-STATE-39` true. Done means each sign and
role row has its stated observable result, the existing test changes stay within G1–G10,
and the PR carries the head's verification and mutation evidence. Full host CI awaits the
coordinator's `ci:full` after the judges, as the dispatch instructions require. Linux remains
unproved under `D13` and M8-U97.

L0 hands containment signs to L1 through a callback before attempting their events. L1
keeps those signs under the dispatch's entry, even when the event sink refuses their
observations. A successful whole-table read can clear an unended identity; an unreadable
identity cannot. A reaper retries confirmed identities and retains unread scopes until a
successful census shows them empty or replaced.

The groups writer owns failed-write memory. It remembers identities omitted from the
last successful record, checks them before a claim or command start, and persists surviving
omissions at normal exit. An unreadable record or unfinished write is read whole before
any reap or rewrite. Successful reaping consumes a readable unfinished write by rewriting
the record with every remaining sign.

L1 checks synchronously immediately before L0's spawn. L3 also checks at the synchronous
claim boundary, stops new claims and dispatches once it holds a sign, and waits for running
commands to settle with their own outcomes. Claimed board moves, escalation notes and
merges continue. An unstarted dispatch refused by a sign is withheld from L2's failure
accounting. The obsolete R32 host-failure mapping and its granted fixture entry go away.
