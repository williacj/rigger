# Launchd template and init's named skip

`D28` chooses a per-user LaunchAgent with `StartInterval` and `RunAtLoad`, without `KeepAlive`.
`ARCHITECTURE.md`, "Triggers", puts its template under `templates/launchd/` and leaves loading the
job to the owner. The template carries placeholders for host-specific paths and timings; its
fixed label is `com.williacj.rigger`.

The architect's placement ruling on #963 keeps `init`'s refusal of unknown template directories
and exempts `launchd` by name. That protects the refusal's purpose: an unrecognised provider's
assets must not land in a directory no provider reads. The shipped-template comparison test
checks that excluding `launchd` leaves the consumer plan unchanged.

The S2 report at #956, Q-i, observed `codex login status` under a LaunchAgent with `PATH` set;
it named no `auth.json` path. The template therefore sets `PATH` alone in its environment.
M6-12a2 (#965) reads the template's label for installation steps, and M6-12b reads it for
`doctor`'s loaded-job check.
