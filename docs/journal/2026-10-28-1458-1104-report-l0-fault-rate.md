## Cases met under F14

- A zero numerator over calls of a system prints a rate of `0`, not "no rate", as the
  architect's ruling on reading 2 says.
- An `outcome` of `null` counts in its system's denominator and not its numerator, as F3's
  "none of ok, failed and timed-out" does for an absent one.
- A `system` of `null` or the empty string counts in neither rate, under F4.
- One run's events recorded in two stretches with another run between them count as one run,
  headed where it first appears (F8's stream).
