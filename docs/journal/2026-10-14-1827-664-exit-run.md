# The M5 exit run, on the candidate engine

#664 ran M5's exit lines E1–E10 against a throwaway fixture, `williacj/rigger-exit-fixture`. The
engine was the tarball of `b9c254a`, installed outside every checkout. Stand-in agents posted
scripted markers, and the run used the production gate App with a key made for the fixture alone.
The evidence is on #664 and its pull request. This entry keeps what the run taught.

**Three departures, each stopped and ruled on by the coordinator (C40, C41, C42/C44).**

- In one `rigger run`, the lane's second merge ended `not merged` with action `ignore`. That
  came about 2 s after the first merge moved `main`. A later `run` merged it.
- After a continuing attempt pushed a new head, L2's settle in the same claim read markers at the
  old head. So no judge was dispatched at the new head until the next invocation. This happened
  twice: for E4, about 6 s after the push, and for E5, about 7 s after.
- A board item added about 20 s before a `run` was absent from both the engine's board read and
  my own.

Each reading of why is a judgment: no event records the mergeability, head or item list the forge
returned. The coordinator holds whether any becomes a card.

**The forge's own pace shaped the run.**

- The fixture's gate runs use `macos-latest`. They waited about 26 minutes behind the account's
  queue of `williacj/rigger` gate runs.
- GitHub's concurrency group cancelled superseded pending runs, 28 of the fixture's 60. Some heads
  therefore never received a `rigger/gate` status before the next event moved them.
- A wait on `rigger/gate` success has to read the head it means, and must not take the newest
  status for granted.

**`gh pr merge` refused on the client side** ("the base branch policy prohibits the merge") and
named no check. The REST merge endpoint returned the forge's own refusal: `Required status check
"check" is failing.`

**The load gate belongs in the wrapper, not in my head.** `setup-board` started at a one-minute
load of 39.34, before my invocation wrapper refused loads over 24. Every invocation after it
started under the bound.
