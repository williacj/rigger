# launchd's structure, and D28

This card proposes two deltas, for the owner to ratify. `ARCHITECTURE.md`'s launchd paragraph,
under "Triggers", now places the per-user LaunchAgent template, how `run` is supervised, the
`ExitTimeOut` bound, `doctor`'s read of a loaded job, the job's `PATH` and its logs. The new `D28`
records two choices, a LaunchAgent over a daemon and `StartInterval` over `KeepAlive`, with a
deferred row for a dedicated OS user and a separate credential (U1 Q8 on #740).

Every measured claim cites a cell of spike #744's report (744#6390300670). The owner ruled that
report enough in U50 on #740, with Q-n and Q-o lost and sleep out (Q-a4). Neither delta states
anything of those cells, nor a restart cadence after a failure under `StartInterval`, which no
cell measured. The `ExitTimeOut` bound and `KeepAlive`'s forge-call cost stay judgments, as the
report and S7 mark them.

The bound's placement came out of the card's attack and recheck. L0 knows no card, so it answers
only the loaded job's fields. The CLI never reads `concurrency`, and L3 never reads `kinds`, so a
function in `src/config/` computes the bound and the CLI only calls it. The delta adds that the
panel counts as never fewer than one, because a card in flight runs at least its maker. Without
that, a kind with no agent judge would give a bound of zero.

Two cards build against this one:

- **M6-12a**, the template and `README.md`'s installation steps. It also carries the change to
  `init`, which today refuses a directory under `templates/` with no asset destination.
- **M6-12b**, `doctor`'s check of a loaded job, through `src/substrate/launchd.mjs`. Its
  acceptance holds what `doctor` prints, for the real path, for the exit timeout against the
  bound, and for no loaded job.
