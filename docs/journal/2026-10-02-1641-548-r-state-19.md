# #548 — what Rigger cannot end, it records and stops waiting on

**Why a row.** `R-STATE-17` says Rigger ends every process in a process group it created, and every
process of its own user working in a dispatch's directory, under `R-STATE-7` to `R-STATE-9`. #545's
engineer judge found two kinds of process Rigger cannot end:
- one it may not signal, such as a setuid child under `sudo`;
- one in an uninterruptible wait, which outlives the kill.

Today a call holding either never settles, and holds its card's slot for good. Architect ruling 15
on #467 has L0 stop waiting after a bound, or at once where every remaining member answers
`EPERM`, and record each such process as one it could not end. The owner chose that under O70. It
changes what must be true, so it takes a row.

**The words.** Ruling 15's suggested row was one sentence past the spec-style ceiling, and said
"this group of rows". The row splits it in two and names `R-STATE-17` in its place, with the
meaning unchanged. It names no mechanism: no signal, no bound's name or value.

**The ids.** `R-STATE-19` is the next unused id in the group, since `R-STATE-18` is on main. `R-STATE-17`
gains "Except as `R-STATE-19` allows," and keeps its id. Read across the whole document, the
citation states nothing `R-STATE-19` does not already make true. The change to what must be true is
`R-STATE-19`'s, and it takes the new id. `R-STATE-10` cites `R-STATE-11` the same way.

**What claims it.** Nothing yet. The matrix shows the row as a gap, and #539's tests will declare
it. Landing it unclaimed is the owner's to permit by merging.

**Revised after round 1.** All three judges found `R-STATE-10` without the exception. A start's kill
of a recorded group runs through the same wait the bound sets, and only `R-STATE-10` binds it. The
architect, who owns ruling 15, gave the fix: `R-STATE-19` names `R-STATE-17` or `R-STATE-10`, and
`R-STATE-10` cites it beside `R-STATE-11`. `R-STATE-10` keeps its id on the same argument as
`R-STATE-17`. The second sentence now reads "That holds only where", closing a looseness the
reviewer found. A third sentence, from the architect's N2, records the group in place of members a
read could not name. Its words echo `R-STATE-12`'s second sentence, but it covers a process not ended,
which `R-STATE-12` does not, so the owner sees it named when ratifying.

**Revised under O71.** The owner widened the third sentence to a dispatch's directory, and added that
Rigger waits on it no further. The directory census, like the group's kill, can fail to read which
processes it holds. The sentence is 28 words.
