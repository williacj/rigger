## What the runs taught

- **A pause stops pulls, not loops.** After `rigger pause`, cards already pulled kept running
  their maker and judge loops. #146 pushed a third head after the canary's first stop.
- **The run's summary is written at exit, to one of two files.** Under launchd, `rigger run` writes
  its lines to `rigger.out` when it exits 0 and to `rigger.err` otherwise. A watch on one file
  alone misses the other.
- **A judge that exits non-zero loops the run.** #106's Codex reviewer received 2,794,431
  characters against a 1,048,576 limit, exited 1 and left no marker. Rigger#1433 carried the fix.
  At `3e325ab8` the same reviewer ruled on the 2,793,015-byte diff and exited 0 twice. No event
  records that it was given the changed paths in place of the lines, so the switch shows only
  indirectly.
- **A maker cannot meet a PR-body-only acceptance item.** #79's added item asked for an inventory
  in the pull request body, and Claude Code refused the maker's `gh pr edit`. `cowrite-7f` wrote
  that item, and should have asked for a committed file.
- **Answering an escalation does not reset rounds.** Rounds are the distinct (head, digest) pairs
  with an agent marker, and a needs-revision at the bound escalates. A comment moves no head, so
  M8-U113's re-judge at an unchanged head would have re-escalated #105 at once. M8-U114 moved the
  head with a catch-up merge of `main`.
- **A judge rules only by the skill, not by an exception comment.** Twice #105's reviewer met every
  item and ruled needs revision on three tests the macOS sandbox cannot run, while the owner's
  exception naming them was on the pull request
  ([154#7115729626](https://github.com/williacj/emend/pull/154#issuecomment-7115729626),
  [154#7117857136](https://github.com/williacj/emend/pull/154#issuecomment-7117857136)). emend#159
  added the three tests to the code-review skill's list. At the next head the same reviewer ruled
  sound ([154#7134439391](https://github.com/williacj/emend/pull/154#issuecomment-7134439391)).
- **A merge can race the gate status it triggers.** The engine decided to merge before the
  `rigger/gate` status its own marker read had turned green. It refused #79 (11-23 22:18:50Z),
  #159 (11-24 03:44:55Z) and #105 (11-24 14:29:09Z) as `failing, rigger/gate`, and each run exited
  1. On #105 the status turned success 13 s after the refusal. Each card merged on the next run.
- **A judge can rule on lines it ruled sound before.** At `ec184132`, #22's pmReviewer flagged the
  Conversation states inventory
  ([145#7118279469](https://github.com/williacj/emend/pull/145#issuecomment-7118279469)). The
  flagged lines were identical at `9e7de11d`, where the same role had ruled sound
  ([145#7117766119](https://github.com/williacj/emend/pull/145#issuecomment-7117766119)). The
  requirements proposal was judged at six heads across both runs before all four judges ruled sound.
- **The Mac slept through launchd intervals** in the canary, around 02:37Z and 04:02Z on 11-20.
  Kickstarts started the runs the interval missed.
