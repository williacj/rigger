# Exit lists for R-RECORD-3 and the L6 rows, and D17 rule 6.3

M7's exit test 4 cited `R-RECORD-3`, whose "which proposals were taken" clause only L6 makes true.
L6 lands in M9 and M10, so a test claiming `R-RECORD-3` at M7 would mark a clause proved that
nothing makes true. The owner ruled in
[M7-U2](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1009#issuecomment-6434513387) item 6 that M7's
exit test cites only `R-RECORD-3`'s landed clauses and that the proposals clause stays with M9.
[M7-K4](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1009#issuecomment-6434590678) read that as
taking `R-RECORD-3` off M7's list, keeping `R-RECORD-2` and `R-RECORD-4` there, and putting
`R-RECORD-3` on M9's list next to `R-IMPROVE-6`.

The second conflict was the architect's
[r2 review](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1009#issuecomment-6434658018), §7. `D17`
rule 6.3 said v0 ships with the set of counted gaps empty, and M8's "Then" list said no counted
gap is left. The `R-IMPROVE` rows are counted gaps that no test can claim before L6 exists, so
both could not hold. The owner ruled in
[M7-U3](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1009#issuecomment-6440291524) item 3 that rule
6.3 is amended so such a requirement closes at its own milestone, and that `R-IMPROVE-2` and
`R-IMPROVE-6` join M9's or M10's exit list.

The card's first pre-dispatch attack found that "a requirement whose layer lands after v0" turns
on a mapping no binding document holds. `R-RECORD-3` is an R-RECORD row with one L6 clause. Its
option (a) bounds the exception by the plan instead: a requirement the exit list of a milestone
after M8 cites closes at that milestone. The card took that wording, so rule 6.3's exception
differs from the owner's words in M7-U3 item 3, and the owner sees that at the marker.

The attack also found that the decisions register's preamble forbids this as an amendment: "A
change to what a ratified rule means is never an amendment: a later decision supersedes it, so the
original stays readable." The owner ruled in
[M7-U4](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1009#issuecomment-6449422492) Q21 (a) that the
change is an amendment by the owner's explicit exception. The preamble itself does not change, and
`D17`'s status records the amendment in the preamble's form.

M9 is where the three ids close, because M9's second bullet is the path that makes them true:
"Anything touching a recorded decision goes to the owner". `R-IMPROVE-2`'s second sentence is that
bullet, so the card fixed it at M9 rather than M10. The new M9 exit test has a decision-touching
move become a proposal, records the proposal and the owner's answer, and has `report` show which
proposals were taken. `R-IMPROVE-6`'s "whether they helped" is read from that same record, since a
proposal cites its signal (`R-IMPROVE-3`), and the record holds that signal before and after the
answer. That reading is the second attack's non-blocking finding 1, option (a), and the owner rules
on it at the marker.
