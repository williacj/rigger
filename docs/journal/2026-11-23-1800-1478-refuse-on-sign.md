# R-STATE-30 to R-STATE-39: refuse to start on a sign

## Why

The architect found that a dispatch can settle with one of its processes alive, and that a later attempt at the same card then starts with no restart and no check for that process. Its ruling withdrew ruling 2's premise that a second attempt only starts after a restart ([1453#7090420413](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1453#issuecomment-7090420413), section 3 put the question).

## The rulings

- **The refusal.** The owner ruled that on one host a later attempt may not start while such a process may still be alive ([1453#7104432562](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1453#issuecomment-7104432562)).
- **What a sign is.** The owner then narrowed that to a sign: a process recorded as not ended and not shown gone, a failed process-table read, or a missing or unreadable record. A process Rigger never saw any sign of stays a stated residual ([1453#7106926111](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1453#issuecomment-7106926111)).
- **The rows and tables.** The owner ratified them as pinned on #1478, put them in M8, and made them bind v0 (M8-U104 to M8-U108, [1293#7110158253](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1293#issuecomment-7110158253) and [1293#7110633947](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/issues/1293#issuecomment-7110633947)).
- **The claim exception.** The rows land with no claiming test under M8-U106, as `R-STATE-29` did under M8-U99. #1479 (M8-44) claims them, and the v0 tag waits for it.
