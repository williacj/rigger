# The C615 directory control's first full listing outlasted its caller's read budget

The control `C615 Mac directory full tool exit census` failed twice on CI at its first-listing guard, `the actual full lsof listing did not answer with the recorded tail cwd`: job [115996503524](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/actions/runs/38605879538/job/115996503524) in 2,223.6 ms and job [116276037538](https://github.com/williacj/rigger/actions/runs/38690813980/job/116276037538) in 2,002.6 ms. Those were the two longest of the 87 CI runs of the target since 2026-10-13; the median was 374.6 ms. Neither log holds a per-read trace, so neither failure is assigned a phase.

The selected cause is source-backed and falsifiable, not a finding about those jobs. The control's caller gave its call `CLEANUP_BOUND`, 1,000 ms, as its read timeout. `sweptNow` starts that budget at the directory sweep, whose first read is the real whole-user `lsof` listing. A listing that outlasts it ends in `ETIMEDOUT`. `runOnce` throws, `sweeping` records the directory unread, and the caller still exits 0. The control then finds no status-0, error-free first answer: cell D2 at its `:3260` guard. An instrumented failure whose first full listing answered in time, or answered and failed a `listsTail` term, would refute it. In twenty consecutive instrumented whole-file runs at base on the maker's host, the control passed every time and its first full listing took 131.8 to 320.9 ms of the 1,000 ms.

Under owner rulings O274 and O275, this caller alone now gives its call 5,000 ms, L0's own `READ_TIMEOUT`. That is a judgment, not a measured CI threshold. The first-answer predicate, the field predicate and every downstream guard are unchanged.

The forcing holds the real first `lsof` listing stopped before it runs, with its own arguments, until a controller has seen it held 2,000 ms. At base the read timed out at 1,000 ms, before release, and the control failed `:3260` five times in a row; at head the held listing answered and the control passed five times in a row. The ledger, the natural no-reproduction sequence and the named controls for each answer and teardown cell are in the pull request that closes #757. What the two CI jobs' first listings actually returned remains unknown.
