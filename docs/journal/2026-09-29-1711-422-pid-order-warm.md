ABOUTME: Journal for #422 (M2-06e): how the two causes the card named were forced at base, and
why each fix is in the test.

# #422 — A pid order the test assumed, and a first exec the test paid inside its window

## What was wrong

**U1** (`test/kill-recorded.test.mjs`) sorted the recorded kills by pid and expected the leader's
record first. The leader starts first, but once pids wrap the member can hold the lower pid, and
the test failed while the product had recorded both kills correctly.

**U2** (`test/process-adapter.test.mjs`) exec'd a command it had just written inside the 1,000 ms
`OUTLIVED` window, the exposure #420 found in its T4. It had not been seen failing.

## How each cause was forced

Neither force needed a sleep or a wait for the host to do something by chance.

- **U1.** Waiting for a real pid wrap would burn about 100,000 pids while other sessions spawn
  too, so the order is not ours to choose that way. Instead, a scratch patch of `startGroup` has
  Perl fork the member first and the leader second. The leader makes the group, and the member
  joins it and runs `tail`. The leader then waits on a FIFO rather than on a child. The two
  processes carry the same names and command lines as at base, so only the pid order differs.
- **U2.** #421's padding: the `command` fixture gets a 250 MB trailing comment, so its first
  exec is held past the window every time.

## The fixes

- **U1.** The kills are sorted by name, which the two differ in, and still compared whole
  against exactly one record for the leader and one for the member.
- **U2.** The command passes through `warmed()` before the adapter spawns it. Its assertions,
  `OUTLIVED`, its `readTimeout` and `SETTLES_WITHIN` are unchanged.

## What taught us

The pre-commit hook runs the whole suite in the worktree, under whatever TMPDIR the commit
inherits. A fresh worktree has no `node_modules`, and the hook then reds three files on the
missing `acorn`. Run `npm ci` in the worktree before the first commit, and pass TMPDIR to the
commit as well as to the test runs.
